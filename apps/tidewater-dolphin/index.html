Tip

Tap Shift to kick; hold it for a steady rhythm. Each kick is a surge, and the glide between kicks is long.

W noses down, S noses up. A and D bank into a turn — tight when slow, wide when fast.

Speed plus a nose-up near the surface breaches. Space bursts to nine metres a second.

R eases the camera back behind you. X brakes to a hover.

With PosePuppet open on the same origin, your body swims the dolphin: kick rhythm, lean, crouch, hands forward to burst.

The settings panel (H) exposes Tidewater's sea state, time of day, clouds, haze and post-processing.
